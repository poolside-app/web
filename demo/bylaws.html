Maple Grove Swim Club bylaws

Article I. Name

The club is Maple Grove Swim Club.

Article II. Purpose

To run a neighborhood pool for its members and their guests.

Article III. Members

A family is a member for the season once its dues are paid, or while it keeps to its payment plan.

Article IV. The board

The board is the President, the Treasurer and the Facilities Director. The board meets once a month during the season.

Article V. Minutes

Minutes are posted for members once the board members who attended have approved them.
