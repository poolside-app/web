🧪 Demo club

Maple Grove Swim Club

A made-up pool club running on Poolside. Click anything: approve a signup, confirm a Venmo, open the gate, text everyone. It all works on screen, and nothing is real: no texts, emails, payments or doors. Wherever a button would do something in a real club, the demo tells you what.

The board side →You're Sam Brooks, the Treasurer: signups, payments, keyfobs, parties, the gate. The member app →You're Jamie Rivera: the home screen, unlock, parties, your family. Sign up as a new family →The signup form, from the club's own page. The club's public page →What neighbors see before they join.
