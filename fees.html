Every fee we charge

All of them, on one page, including the ones most software companies bury. If we charge for it, it is listed here.

The short version: you pay us a flat annual subscription. On top of that, when one of your members pays you by card, we take a small percentage. That is the entire business. There are no per-member fees, no setup requirement, no charge for support, and nothing is gated behind a higher tier.

3. The one fee your members see

A member who would rather spread dues across the season than pay in one go can choose a payment plan. That costs them $4 per payment, capped at $16 for the entire plan — the way an installment plan works on insurance or a utility bill.

  • Your books do not move. Each installment is the pure dues figure. The fee rides alongside and comes to us, so your club nets exactly the same whether a household pays once or six times.
  • Paying in full avoids it entirely. It is a fee for the convenience, not a fee for paying.
  • The cap is the point. Without it, a flat fee across an eight-payment plan would take $32 on $600 of dues — over 5%, which is the sort of number that makes a board tell its members to write checks instead.

4. What Stripe takes, which is not ours

Card processing costs 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction in the US. That goes to Stripe, not to Poolside. We mention it because it is the largest number on this page and you should not be surprised by it on your statement.

You can pass that cost to members who choose to pay by card — a $600 dues bill then shows as $618.23 on a card and $600.00 by Venmo. One switch in Settings → Payments.

Worth knowing: that pass-through covers Stripe's fee only. Our percentage always comes out of the club's side, never the member's. We are not going to quietly add our cut to a member's bill and let you find out about it later.

6. What we never charge for

  • Per member or per contact. The tiers are capacity bands, not headcount meters. 74 households or 200, the price is the price.
  • Features. Every tier gets every feature. No "upgrade to Pro to send SMS".
  • Extra board members or admins. Add the whole board.
  • Support. Email a person, get a person.
  • Getting your data out. Export any time, including on the way out the door.
  • Text messages and emails, within your plan's annual allowance — which is sized so that a club sending normal reminders never touches it.
  • Canceling. No termination fee, no notice period, no exit interview.

7. Things a club sets, which we do not take a cut of

Some fees on your Poolside account are yours. You set them, you keep them, and they never appear on our invoice:

  • Late fees. You choose the amount, the due date and the grace period, and you can waive any of them in one click. Charged once per household per season, never more.
  • Your own reactivation fee on a lapsed payment plan.
  • Guest pass and party prices, membership tiers, program fees — all yours to set.

Money-back, no drama

If you are not happy in the first 30 days, email us and we will refund your subscription. No forms, no exit interview. We would rather you found the right tool than felt stuck in this one.
