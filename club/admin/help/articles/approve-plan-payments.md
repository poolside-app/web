---
title: "How do I approve this month's plan payments?"
category: "Money"
---

Poolside never charges a payment-plan payment on its own. Someone on the board approves each one first.

### Approve them

1. Go to **Money → Upcoming**.
2. Under **Next 30 days**, check each family's payment.
3. Tap **Approve** on each one you're happy to charge. Tapped one by mistake? Tap **Undo**.

Approved payments are charged on your club's billing day. **Last 30 days** shows what was charged and anything that was declined. **Show the whole season** shows every plan payment still to come.

### Reminders

Whoever handles payments gets a reminder on **Home**, and a pop-up, 3 days before the billing day, and every day after it while anything is still waiting.

### Declined cards

Approving a payment also covers its retries. If the card is declined, Poolside tries again 3, 7 and 14 days later without asking you again. A plan is never ended over a payment the board hadn't approved.

> Tip: Payments a family makes themselves, like the first payment at signup, aren't listed here. They don't need approval.
