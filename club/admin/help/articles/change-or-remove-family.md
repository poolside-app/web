---
title: "How do I change a family's details or remove a family?"
category: "Signups & Families"
---

### Find the family

1. Go to **Members → Households**.
2. Type a name, address or phone number in the search box, or tap one of the counts at the top: families, paid, on a plan, unpaid.
3. Tap the family to open **Edit household**.

### Change their details

Change what you need, like the address, a phone number or their membership level, and tap **Save changes**.

Families can change most of this themselves in the app, under **My family**.

### Dues paid

Tick **Dues paid for current year** if they paid you outside Poolside, like a check. For a family on a payment plan, this box is set by their plan and can't be changed by hand. Plan payments are card only.

### Removing a family

- **Remove household** takes them off your member list. They can't sign in, but their history is kept, and they show under the removed count.
- **Permanently delete** erases the family and everything about them. It can't be undone.

> Tip: When a family leaves the club, use **Remove household**, not **Permanently delete**. You'll want their history if they come back.
