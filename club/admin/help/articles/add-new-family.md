---
title: "How do I add a family myself?"
category: "Signups & Families"
---

Most families sign up themselves on your signup page. Sometimes you need to add one by hand, like a family who paid cash at the pool.

### Have them sign up (best)

Send them your signup link. Find it on **Settings → Season** under **Your signup page**, and tap **Copy link**. They fill in their own details and sign the policies, so there's nothing for you to type.

### Add them yourself

1. Go to **Members → Households**.
2. Tap **+ Add household**.
3. Fill in the **Family name**, **Membership level**, and the address.
4. Under **Members**, add each person with their **Name**, **Phone** and **Email**. Anyone with a phone or email can sign in to the app.
5. Tick **Dues paid for current year** if they've already paid.
6. Tap **Create household**.

### Adding many families at once

If you have a spreadsheet of families, use **📤 Import CSV** on **Members → Households**. See "How do I import families from a spreadsheet?"

> Tip: A family added by hand hasn't signed your policies. Ask them to sign up themselves instead when you can.
