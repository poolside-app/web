---
title: "What do I do about a lost or broken fob?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

### Lost

When a family reports a fob lost in the app, it shows under **🚫 Lost fobs to turn off** on **Members → Keyfobs & Access**.

1. Turn the fob off on your doors. If your club still uses the door company's program, turn it off there. If Poolside runs your doors, the next **Send to the doors** turns it off.
2. Tap **Turned off**.

If they asked for a replacement, it shows under **🔑 Fobs to issue** once it's paid.

### Broken

A family with a fob that doesn't work asks through **Ask the board**. Check it at the door. If it's broken, find it under **All fobs** and tap **Broken** to swap it, free or for your club's fee, then issue the new one.

### Turning off any fob

Under **All fobs**, tap **Turn off** on the fob.

> Tip: Change how many fobs are free, the fee for another one and the most a family can have on the same page: **Free fobs for a new family**, **Fee for another fob ($)** and **Most fobs per family**.
