---
title: "How much time does Poolside save us?"
category: "Board & Settings"
---
Go to **Insights → Time saved**. It adds up the work the board used to do by hand that Poolside now does: setting up the signup form, typing signups into a spreadsheet, checking Venmo and PayPal, turning keyfobs on and off, booking parties, and trips to the pool to let someone in.

### How it's counted

Each row shows how many times Poolside did that job for your club, how many minutes it used to take, and the total. Only things that actually happened in Poolside count. Keyfobs brought over from the old door program don't count.

The dollar figure is what it would have cost if the club paid someone to do the work, at the hourly rate shown.

### Changing the estimates

If you can use Settings, each row's minutes and the hourly rate can be changed right on the page. Tap **Save** when you're done.

> Tip: It's a good number to share at the annual meeting.
