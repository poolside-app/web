---
title: "How do I put the board app on my phone and get pop-ups?"
category: "Getting Started"
---

Poolside works best as an app on your phone's Home Screen. That's also the only way an iPhone will show pop-ups.

### Add it to your Home Screen

On an iPhone, in Safari:

1. Open your club's board sign-in link in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
4. Open Poolside from the new icon. It opens already signed in.

On Android, in Chrome:

1. Open the board sign-in link in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap the three dots, then "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").

### Turn on pop-ups

1. Open the app from your Home Screen.
2. On **Home**, tap **Turn on pop-ups**, then allow notifications when your phone asks.

You'll then get a pop-up when something is for you: a member question for your position, a fob to issue, a payment to approve, a party booked, and so on.

### Board member and a member too?

You only need one app. It opens to the board side whenever you're signed in as a board member. To see what members see, tap **Member view**.

> Tip: If pop-ups stop, open the app once and check **Home** for the **Turn on pop-ups** button again.
