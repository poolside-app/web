---
title: "How do I close the pool for the season?"
category: "Seasons & Renewals"
---

When the pool closes, one setting changes everything members see.

### Close it

1. Go to **Settings → Season**.
2. Set **Season is** to **Closed for the off-season**.
3. Optionally type your own message for when you're closed. Leave it blank for "Thanks for a great season! We look forward to seeing you next summer."
4. Tap **Save changes**.

### What changes

- The member app and your club's page show your message instead of today's hours.
- The calendar stops showing open hours.
- Members can't unlock the gate from the app.
- **Home** says "Closed for the season".

New families can still sign up while the pool is closed. Your signup page shows your message above the form. To stop new signups, set **Memberships are** to **Frozen — block new applications**.

### Keyfobs

Closing the season takes every member's keyfob off the doors. Board fobs, and every other level, stay on.

- With the nightly keyfob sync on, it happens that night by itself.
- Otherwise, whoever handles keyfobs gets a to-do. Go to **Members → Keyfobs & Access → Keyfob sync**, tap **Sync keyfobs now**, check the list, then tap **Send to the doors**.

In the spring, setting **Season is** back to **Open** puts the fobs of families who've paid back on the same way. A family who hasn't paid stays off until they do.

### Don't press "Close and start next season"

That button is for moving signups and renewals to next year. It doesn't close the pool. See "How do I start selling next season?"

> Tip: In the spring, set **Season is** back to **Open — show normal home page** and the hours come back by themselves.
