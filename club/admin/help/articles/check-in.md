---
title: "How does the gate check-in page work?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

The check-in page is for whoever sits at the gate: a lifeguard, a volunteer, or a shared iPad. It shows only check-in, with no member contact details and no money.

### Set up the gate iPad or a lifeguard

1. Go to **Settings → Board**.
2. Under **Lifeguard and gate logins**, tap **Add a lifeguard or gate login**.
3. Sign in on the iPad with that login. It opens straight to the check-in page.

Board members can open it from **Settings → Gate & check-in**, with **Open the check-in page →**.

### Check a family in

1. Type part of the family name, or the last 4 digits of a phone number.
2. Each family shows **✓ Paid** or **⚠ Unpaid**.
3. Set how many family members and guests came in, and tap **✓ Check in**.

The top of the page counts who's in the pool and how many came today.

> Tip: An unpaid family can still be checked in; the button says so. Point them to the board, or to the app to pay.
