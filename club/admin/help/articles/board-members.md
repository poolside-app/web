---
title: "How do I add a board member and choose what they see?"
category: "Getting Started"
---

Each board member signs in with their own phone number or email. What they see depends on the board position they hold. The President, or anyone with full access, does this on **Settings → Board**.

### Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings → Board**.
2. Open **Invite someone new**.
3. Fill in **Their name** and **Their mobile number (we text the invite)**, or **Or their email**.
4. Tap **Send invite**.

They get a text or email with a link to sign in. There's no password; they sign in with a code sent to their phone or email.

### Give them a position

Positions are the jobs on your board, like Treasurer or Facilities Director. Each one has:

- **Job description**: what the job is, shown to the person who holds it.
- **Gets told about**: the alerts it receives, like member questions about keyfobs, or parties being booked.
- **Can use**: the screens they can open. **Everything (full access, like the President)** gives them every screen.

To put someone in a position, open the position, tap **Who holds it**, and pick them. Their screens change the next time they open the app. Use **+ Add a position** for a job your board has that isn't listed.

If a position is empty, its alerts go to the President.

### When someone leaves the board

On **Settings → Board**, find them under **Board members** and tap **Remove from the board**. Their sign-in stops working right away. Everything they did stays in the **Audit log**.

### Lifeguards and the gate iPad

Under **Lifeguard and gate logins**, **Add a lifeguard or gate login** creates a login that only shows the check-in page and that person's own lifeguard shifts. No member contact details and no money. Use one for a shared iPad at the gate, or one per lifeguard.

> Tip: Always keep at least two people with full access, so the board is never locked out if one person loses their phone.
