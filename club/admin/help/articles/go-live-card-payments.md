---
title: "How do I turn on card payments?"
category: "Money"
---

Poolside uses Stripe to take card payments. The money goes straight to your club's own Stripe account, then to your club's bank.

### What you'll need

- Your club's tax ID (EIN). If your club doesn't have one, a board member can use their own Social Security number, but Stripe then reports the income under that person's name.
- Your club's bank account and routing number.
- One board member's name, home address and date of birth, for Stripe's identity check.

### Connect Stripe

1. Go to **Money → Payments → Money setup → Card payments (Stripe)**.
2. Tap **Connect Stripe** and fill in Stripe's forms. It takes a few minutes; Stripe sometimes needs a day to review.
3. Back in Poolside, tap **Refresh status**. If you stopped partway, tap **Resume onboarding**.

### Card fees

Stripe charges about 2.9% + 30¢ per card payment. Tick **Pass the Stripe processing fee to members who pay by card** to add it to what card payers are charged. Venmo, check and cash stay the regular price.

### Test payments

While **Simulate all payments — turn off before real members start paying** is ticked, no real money moves. Card checkouts are pretend, and there's a button to pretend a Venmo arrived. Untick it before real families pay.

> Tip: Connect Stripe well before renewal season, so your first payout has time to arrive.
