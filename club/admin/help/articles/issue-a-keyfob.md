---
title: "How do I give a family a keyfob?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

Families ask for fobs in the app. A new family's first fob is free if your club gives one. Another fob, or a replacement, is paid for in the app before it reaches you.

### Issue a fob

1. Go to **Members → Keyfobs & Access**.
2. Under **🔑 Fobs to issue**, find the family. The oldest requests are first.
3. Program the fob for your doors. If your club still uses the door company's own program, add it there first.
4. Type the number printed on the fob in **Number on the fob**, and tap **Issue**.

Fobs print their number two ways, like 0000786777 or 012,00345. Either works. Poolside won't let you enter a number that's already someone else's.

### A fob that's waiting on payment

Requests not paid yet are under **⏳ waiting on payment**. One that says "Venmo to confirm" is ready once you've checked Venmo; issuing it marks it paid.

### Fobs a family already has

To add a fob a family was given before Poolside, open **＋ Add a fob a family already has**, pick the family, type the number and tap **Add**. It's on right away.

### Looking up a fob

Under **All fobs**, search by family, address or fob number.

> Tip: Keyfob requests go to whoever holds the board position that's told about keyfobs, usually the Facilities Director. They get a pop-up for each one.
