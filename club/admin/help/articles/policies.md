---
title: "How do I change the policies families agree to?"
category: "Seasons & Renewals"
---

Policies are the rules families accept when they sign up or renew, like pool rules or the waiver.

### Change or add one

1. Go to **Members → Policies**.
2. Tap **Edit** on a policy, or **+ Add policy** for a new one.
3. Fill in the title and the body.
4. Tick **Required to apply (gates the signup form)** if families must accept it before they can sign up.
5. Tap **Save**.

Use the arrows to change the order. **Archive** retires a policy without deleting it.

### Who sees the change

New signups and renewals see the new wording from now on. Families who already signed this season aren't asked again until they renew. If your club backs up signups to Google Drive, each signed form there keeps the exact wording they agreed to.

> Tip: Change policies before sending the renewal message, so everyone renewing agrees to this season's rules.
