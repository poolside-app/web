---
title: "How do I change the pool's hours?"
category: "Board & Settings"
---

1. Go to **Settings → Pool location & hours**.
2. Set **Usually opens at** and **Usually closes at**.
3. If some days are different, open **Different hours on some days?** and set those days, or tick **Closed**.
4. Tap **Save changes**.

Members see today's hours on their home screen, and each day's hours on the calendar.

### One-off closures

For a single day, like a holiday or a swim meet, add an event with the kind **🚫 Closure** instead. See "How do I add an event or a closure to the calendar?"

### Closed for the season

Use **Settings → Season** instead. See "How do I close the pool for the season?"

> Tip: Pool hours are what members see. Who can get in, and when, is set separately on **Members → Keyfobs & Access → Access**.
