---
title: "How does refer-a-friend work?"
category: "Money"
---

Members can invite another family from the app, under **My family**. When the new family joins with that link and pays, the new family saves money, and the member who invited them earns a reward.

### Set the amounts

On **Money → Payments → Money setup**, under **Referral program**, set **The member earns ($)** and **The new family saves ($)**, then tap **Save referral amounts**.

### Paying the reward

The reward unlocks 30 days after the new family pays, and it's never more than the price of the member's own membership. The member picks how they'd like it: money back this season, or a credit toward next season's dues. Unlocked rewards show under **💌 Referral rewards** for whoever handles money.

1. Tap **Approve** (with the amount), or **Decline** if it shouldn't be paid.
2. For a credit, that's it. It comes off their next dues.
3. For money back, send it: tap the "💳 Refund to their card" button if they paid by card, or send it yourself and tap **Sent by Venmo** or **Sent by check**. **Don't send** cancels it.

A reward for your own family is approved and sent by another board member.

> Tip: A family that has been a member before doesn't get the new-family discount through a referral link.
