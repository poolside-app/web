---
title: "How many texts do we get?"
category: "News, Photos & Texts"
---

Your plan includes a yearly allowance of texts for club-wide messages. **Settings → Plan** shows what's left.

### What counts

Only messages you choose to send to everyone: **💬 Text all members**, and the renewal message when you tick **💬 Text**.

### What doesn't count

Sign-in codes, welcome texts when a family is approved, passes, and a family member's link to get the app. These are always free.

### If you run out

Buy more on **Settings → Plan**: 3,000 texts for $100. They're used only after your allowance is gone, and they never expire.

### The daily limit

Texts to a lot of families can hit the day's sending limit. When that happens, the rest go the next morning by themselves, and the screen tells you how many are waiting.

> Tip: A text is 160 characters. Emoji and curly quotes make it cost double, so keep big messages plain.
