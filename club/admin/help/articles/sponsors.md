---
title: "How do I add a sponsor?"
category: "News, Photos & Texts"
---

1. Go to **Content → Sponsors**.
2. Tap **+ Add sponsor**.
3. Fill in the business name, **Logo URL**, **Website / link** and a short **Description (shown in the popup; keep it short)**.
4. Tick **Active** and tap **Save**.

Active sponsors show on the member app and your club's page.

### The sponsor pop-up

Under **Sponsor pop-up**, tick **Show a sponsor pop-up when people open the app** and pick **How often**. Each time, it shows one of your active sponsors.

> Tip: **Paid through (optional reminder)** reminds you when a sponsor's year is up.
