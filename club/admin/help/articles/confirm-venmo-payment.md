---
title: "How do I confirm a Venmo, check or cash payment?"
category: "Money"
---

Card payments confirm themselves. Venmo, checks and cash need someone on the board to check the money arrived and mark it paid.

### Venmo: match the code

Every "Pay with Venmo" button fills in the Venmo note for the family, ending in a 4-character code like #K7Q2. The same code shows next to the family in Poolside, so you can match a payment even when the name on the Venmo account is different.

1. Go to **Money → Payments** and open **💰 Waiting for payment**.
2. Under **💸 Venmo to check**, open Venmo and look for each code in the payment notes.
3. Tick the ones you see, then tap **✓ Confirm selected**.

Each one gets its own next step: a signup is approved and marked paid, a party is booked, a keyfob is ready to issue. The family is told right away.

### One at a time

- A new family: on **Members → Signups**, tap **✓ Approve & verify Venmo** on their row, or "✓ Verify Venmo received" if they're already approved.
- A party: on **Calendar → Parties**, open it and tap **✓ Verify Venmo + book**.
- A keyfob: on **Members → Keyfobs & Access**, a fob that says "Venmo to confirm" is ready once you've seen the money. Issuing it marks it paid.

### A check or cash

Under **💰 Waiting for payment**, tap **Mark paid** on the family's row. It sends them the same confirmation as a Venmo.

### Payment plans

Plan payments can't be paid by Venmo, check or cash. They're card only.

> Tip: Check Venmo before you tap. Confirming a payment tells the family right away.
