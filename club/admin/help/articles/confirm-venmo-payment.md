---
title: "How do I confirm a Venmo, check or cash payment?"
category: "Money"
---

Card payments confirm themselves. Venmo, checks and cash need someone on the board to check the money arrived and mark it paid.

### A new family's dues

- Still waiting for approval: on **Members → Pipeline**, tap **✓ Approve & verify Venmo**. It approves them and marks them paid.
- Already approved: tap "✓ Verify Venmo received" on their row.

### Several at once

1. Go to **Money → Payments** and open **💰 Payments — pending verify + open balances**.
2. Tick the families whose payments you've seen, then tap **✓ Verify selected**. Or tap **Mark paid** on one row.

### A party

On **Calendar → Parties**, open the party and tap **✓ Verify Venmo + book**. That books the date.

### A keyfob

On **Members → Keyfobs & Access**, a fob that says "Venmo to confirm" is ready once you've seen the money. Issuing it marks it paid.

### Payment plans

Plan payments can't be paid by Venmo, check or cash. They're card only.

> Tip: Check Venmo before you tap. Marking something paid sends the family their confirmation right away.
