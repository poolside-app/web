---
title: "What does Sync keyfobs now do?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

**Sync keyfobs now** puts Poolside's keyfobs on your doors: newly issued fobs, fobs that were turned off or lost, and the hours and dates from **Access**.

### It checks first

1. Go to **Members → Keyfobs & Access → Keyfob sync**.
2. Tap **Sync keyfobs now**. The gate computer at the pool reads the doors, and Poolside shows exactly what would change. Open **See each change** for the list.
3. If it looks right, tap **Send to the doors**.

Nothing is written until you tap **Send to the doors**. Afterwards Poolside reads the doors back to confirm each change took.

### What it never touches

Fobs Poolside doesn't manage are left alone. Changes go on one fob at a time, so the doors keep working the whole time.

### If your club also uses the door company's program

That program keeps its own list. Uploading from it can undo what Poolside sent. Use one or the other, and ask Poolside before switching.

> Tip: It needs the gate computer at the pool to be on and up to date. If it isn't, this section says so.
