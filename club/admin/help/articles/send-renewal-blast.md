---
title: "How do I send the renewal message to last season's families?"
category: "Seasons & Renewals"
---

When next season goes on sale, send last season's families their renewal. Each family gets its own link that opens their renewal with everything filled in. They accept the policies, sign, and pay in full or with a payment plan. No password needed.

### Send it

1. Go to **Members → Renewals**.
2. Check the season and how many families haven't renewed yet.
3. Under **Send by**, pick **📧 Email**, **💬 Text**, or both.
4. Change the **Message** if you like. Each family's own link is added after it.
5. Tap **Send renewal message →**.

### Who gets it

Only families who were members last season and haven't renewed, sent to the person who opened each account. Send it again later and it only reaches the families who still haven't renewed.

### One-tap renewal

Families who ticked "Make next season's renewal one tap" are sent their renewal to approve automatically when the season goes on sale, with a reminder a week later. Their saved card is charged only when they approve.

### Early-bird discount

Make a code on **Money → Payments → Money setup → Discounts** with a last day, and tick **Show it on the member app's home screen**. Families type it in **Have a discount code?** when they renew.

> Tip: Each text counts toward your club's yearly text allowance (**Settings → Plan** shows what's left). If the day's text limit is reached, the rest go the next morning by themselves, and the screen says so.
