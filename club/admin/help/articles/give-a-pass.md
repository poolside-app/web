---
title: "How do I give someone a pass?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

A pass opens extra doors for a set time, like the pump room for a volunteer or every door for a pool tech. Any board member can give one.

### Give a pass

1. On **Home**, tap **Passes**. (It's also on **Members → Keyfobs & Access**.)
2. Tap **Give a pass**.
3. Choose who it's for:
   - **A member family**: it opens from their app. Tick **Text it to them too (they always get a pop-up)** to also send a text.
   - **Someone else**, like a landscaper: fill in **Their name** and **Their mobile**. We text them a private link that opens the doors during the pass. They don't need the app.
4. Pick the **Doors**, or **All doors**.
5. Set **From** and the end time, and add a **Note (optional)**.
6. Tap **Give the pass**.

You'll see **Pass given.**, and for someone else, **Their link (to send another way if needed)**.

### Ending a pass early

Open **Passes** and tap **End now**. The link stops working right away.

### Parties

A paid party gets your club's party doors for the day automatically. You don't need to give a pass.

> Tip: Passes open doors from a phone. They don't put anything on a keyfob.
