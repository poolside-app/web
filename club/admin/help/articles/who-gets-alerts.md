---
title: "Who gets which alert, and how do I change it?"
category: "Getting Started"
---

Poolside sends each alert to the board member whose job it is, as a pop-up and under **Needs you** on **Home**. A new signup goes to whoever handles signups, a keyfob request to whoever handles keyfobs, a refund to whoever handles payments, and so on.

### Change who gets what

1. Go to **Settings → Board**.
2. Open a position, like Treasurer or Facilities Director.
3. Under **Gets told about**, tick the alerts this position should get.
4. Tap **Who holds it** to put the right person in the position.

### When nobody holds a position

Its alerts go to the President, then the Vice-President. The President, and anyone with full access, can see every to-do on **Home**.

### Member questions

Questions from **Ask the board** follow the same rule. See "How do I answer a member's question?"

> Tip: If someone gets alerts that aren't theirs, check which positions they hold. Every position they hold sends them its alerts.
