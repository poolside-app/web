---
title: "How do I text all members?"
category: "News, Photos & Texts"
---

Use this for urgent news, like the pool closing today for weather. It goes to every adult with a phone number on file.

### Send it

1. Go to **Content → Announcements**.
2. Tap **💬 Text all members**.
3. Type the **Message**. Keep it short; it shows how many texts it will use.
4. Tap **Queue for approval**.

### Another board member sends it

Texts can't be unsent, so another board member has to release it. They see it on **Content → Announcements** and tap **Send it now →**. If you're your club's only board member, you can release it yourself.

> Tip: Every text counts toward your club's monthly text allowance. For news that isn't urgent, post an announcement with **🔔 Notify members (a pop-up on their phones)** instead.
