---
title: "How do I back up signups to Google Drive?"
category: "Board & Settings"
---

Poolside can save a copy of every signup and signed renewal to your club's Google Drive: a PDF of the signed form, and a row in a Google Sheet with every adult's and child's name, the address, phone numbers (like 408-555-1234) and how they paid. Each season gets its own folder and tab, for example "2027 Sign-ups", even for families who sign up the fall before.

The same sheet has a Parties tab at the bottom: one row per party, with its date, family, guests, amount and status, and every step that happened to it, like "Nov 4: approved · Nov 5: paid by card $150.00 · Nov 20: canceled by the family". Canceled and turned-down parties stay on it, and so does a party whose family was deleted from Poolside.

Each night Poolside copies anything that was missed and brings the Parties tab up to date. A new signup is copied the moment it's sent.

### Connect it

1. Go to **Settings → Backups & archive**.
2. Tap **Connect Google Drive**. Google asks which account: pick your club's, not your own.

Within a minute, a "Poolside Archive" folder appears in that Drive with the roster sheet and every signup so far. From then on, each new signup is copied over on its own.

### Switch to a different Google account

Tap **Disconnect**, then **Connect Google Drive**, and pick the other account. Everything is copied into a fresh "Poolside Archive" folder there. The old account keeps its copy.

### If something didn't copy

Settings → Backups & archive shows the last error. Anything that didn't copy is tried again that night, with nothing to press.

> Tip: This is a record of who signed up and what they signed. It isn't a backup of everything in Poolside.
