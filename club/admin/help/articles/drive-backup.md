---
title: "How do I back up signups to Google Drive?"
category: "Board & Settings"
---

Poolside can save a copy of every signup and signed renewal to your club's Google Drive: a PDF of the signed form, and a row in a Google Sheet with every adult's and child's name, the address, phone numbers (like 408-555-1234) and how they paid. Each season gets its own folder and tab, for example "2027 Sign-ups", even for families who sign up the fall before.

### Connect it

1. Go to **Settings → Backups & archive**.
2. Tap **Connect Google Drive**. Google asks which account: pick your club's, not your own.

From then on, each new signup is copied over on its own.

### Switch to a different Google account

Tap **Disconnect**, then **Connect Google Drive**, and pick the other account. New signups go to a fresh "Poolside Archive" folder there. What was already copied stays in the old account.

### If something didn't copy

Tap **Retry failed syncs**, or **🔄 Sync missing applications** to copy any signups that are missing.

> Tip: This is a record of who signed up and what they signed. It isn't a backup of everything in Poolside.
