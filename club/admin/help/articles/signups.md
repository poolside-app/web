---
title: "How do sign-ups work?"
category: "Calendar & Parties"
---

Sign-ups are how the board asks families for help: a shift at an event, or a job like fixing a sprinkler. Each one can come with a thank-you. Any board member can post one.

### Post one

On **Calendar → Sign-ups**, tap **+ Shift** or **+ Job**.

- A shift has a start and end time and how many people you need. For example, the Snack Shack at Saturday's meet, 10 to 2, 3 people.
- A job has a do-by date (or none) and usually one person. Add photos so they know what to fix and where.

Then pick the **Thank-you**: **Nothing**, **A line** (like "Free lunch at the meet"), or **Money off** next season, in dollars or a percent. Tap **Save**.

### What families see

Families get a Sign-ups tab in their app with a number on it: how many they could still take. There's no pop-up and no text. They pick who in the family is doing it, and you get a to-do saying who signed up.

### When a job is done

They tap **I'm done** in the app, with a photo of the finished job if they have one. You get a to-do to check it. Look at their photo and tap **Approve**, or **Send back** with a note on what's left. The board member who posted it approves, or the President.

### After a shift

The morning after, you get a to-do. Open it, untick anyone who didn't come, and tap **Save who came**.

### The thank-you

When you approve:

- Money off becomes a code for that family, shown in their app and emailed to them. They use it when they renew. It's listed with your other discount codes.
- A thank-you goes on the club news with their photo, like "Thank you, Rivera family!" Thank-yous never push your own announcements off the news.

### From a member's question

If a member reports something someone could fix, like a broken sprinkler, open it in Member help and tap **Post as a job**. The job form opens with their words and their photo.

### Turning sign-ups on or off

They're on **Settings → Features**, under **Sign-ups**.
