---
title: "How do I cancel or refund a party or a keyfob?"
category: "Money"
---

### A party

1. On **Calendar → Parties**, open the party.
2. Tap **Cancel booking**. The time is freed and the family's party doors are taken away.
3. If they paid by card, refund it in your Stripe account (see "How do I refund a payment?"). If they paid by Venmo, send it back in Venmo.

If a family pays by card for a party after it was released for not being paid in time, Poolside books it again when the time is still free. If the party was canceled or turned down, whoever handles parties gets a to-do to refund it.

### A keyfob

A fob request can be canceled by the family or the board only before it's paid. Once a fob is paid for:

- If it hasn't been issued, issue it, or refund the payment in Stripe and turn the fob off under **All fobs**.
- If a family paid for a request that was canceled meanwhile, whoever handles payments gets a to-do to refund it. Poolside never refunds on its own.

> Tip: Every card refund you make in Stripe shows up as a to-do in Poolside, so the board can check nothing else needs changing.
