---
title: "How do I take board minutes?"
category: "News, Photos & Texts"
---

### Before the meeting

1. Go to **Content → Board minutes**.
2. Open **Create agenda**, add the items, and tap **Send to the board**. Board members get it as a pop-up.

### During the meeting

1. Tap **▶ Start a meeting**.
2. Mark **Who's here?**.
3. Take **Notes** as you go.
4. For each vote, tap **+ Add motion**, then **✓ Passed**, **✗ Failed** or **⏸ Tabled**.
5. Add **+ Add follow-up** for anything someone needs to do.
6. Tap **💾 Save** as you go, and **✓ Close meeting** at the end.

### Who can see them

**👥 Members** (the default) puts the minutes on your club's **Bylaws & board minutes** page when you close the meeting. Only signed-in members and the board can open that page. **🔒 Board only** keeps them for the board, for things like legal matters.

> Tip: Your bylaws live on the same page, under **📜 Bylaws**.
