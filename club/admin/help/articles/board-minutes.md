---
title: "How do I take board minutes?"
category: "News, Photos & Texts"
---

### Before the meeting

1. Go to **Content → Board minutes**.
2. Open **Create agenda**, add the items, and tap **Send to the board**. Board members get it as a pop-up.

### During the meeting

1. Tap **▶ Start a meeting**.
2. Mark **Who's here?**.
3. Take **Notes** as you go.
4. For each vote, tap **+ Add motion**, then **✓ Passed**, **✗ Failed** or **⏸ Tabled**.
5. Add **+ Add follow-up** for anything someone needs to do.
6. Tap **💾 Save** as you go, and **✓ Close meeting** at the end.

### Getting them approved

1. After **✓ Close meeting**, fix anything that needs it and tap **💾 Save changes**.
2. Tap **📨 Submit for approval**. Each board member you ticked in **Who's here?** gets a pop-up and an "Approve minutes" to-do. You count as approving, since you wrote them.
3. Each of them opens the minutes and taps **✓ Approve**, or **✏️ Request changes** with a note. A request sends them back to you to fix and submit again; everyone is asked again.
4. The moment the last one approves, members can read them. The meeting shows how many have approved and who's left.

If someone hasn't approved after 7 days, the President can tap **Approve for** them; it's recorded. To change minutes that are already approved, edit them and tap **💾 Save changes**: the change goes through the same approval, and members see the approved version until then.

### Who can see them

**👥 Members** (the default) puts the approved minutes on your club's **Bylaws & board minutes** page. Only signed-in members and the board can open that page. **🔒 Board only** keeps them for the board, for things like legal matters; they're approved the same way but never shown to members.

> Tip: Your bylaws live on the same page, under **📜 Bylaws**.
