---
title: "How do I set up a program like swim lessons?"
category: "Calendar & Parties"
---

1. Go to **Calendar → Programs**.
2. Tap **+ New program**.
3. Fill in the name, **Description**, **Who can sign up**, **Capacity**, **Price ($)**, the **Weekdays** and times, the **Start date** and **End date**, the **Instructor** and the **Location**.
4. Tap **Save**.

Members sign up in the app and pay by card there. To see who's signed up, tap **Roster**. For someone who paid another way, tap **Mark paid** on their row.

When the program is over, tap **Archive**.

> Tip: Set **Capacity** so a class can't overfill.
