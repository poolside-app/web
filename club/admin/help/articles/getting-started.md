Welcome to the board. Here's how Poolside is laid out, and what to do first.

### Your board home

When you sign in you land on **Home**. It shows:

- **Needs you**: the things waiting on you, like a signup to review or a fob to issue. Tap one to go straight to it.
- **Today at the pool**: hours, events, parties and lifeguard shifts for today and the week.
- **Quick actions**: shortcuts to the screens your board position uses most.
- The gate buttons, if your club opens doors from the app.

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **Home**, the sections you use, and **More** for everything else. On a computer, the same sections run across the top: **Members**, **Money**, **Calendar**, **Content**, **Settings** and **Insights**. You only see the ones your board position can use.

### First things to do

1. Put the board app on your phone and turn on pop-ups, so you hear about things meant for you. See "How do I put the board app on my phone and get pop-ups?"
2. Read your job. On **Home**, open **Your job as** to see what your position is responsible for.
3. Look at **Needs you** once or twice a week. Anything there is waiting on you.

### Setting up a new club

If your club is just starting on Poolside, **Home** shows a setup checklist at the top. Each line opens the screen where you do it, and it ticks itself off when it's done. Tap **Hide for now** to put it away; it comes back until everything's done.

> Tip: Looking around is safe. Buttons that change money, doors or family records ask you to confirm first.
