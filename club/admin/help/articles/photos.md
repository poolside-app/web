---
title: "How do photos work?"
category: "News, Photos & Texts"
---

Photos show in a slideshow on the member app and your club's page.

### Approving member photos

Members can add photos from the app. They wait for approval under **⏰ Photos pending member-upload approval** on **Content → Photos**. Tap **✓ Approve** or **✕ Reject**.

### Managing the gallery

Tap a photo to add a **Caption (optional, shown overlay)**, move it with **← Move earlier** and **Move later →**, or tap **Remove from gallery**.

### Only the newest 30 are kept

Adding or approving a photo deletes the oldest ones beyond 30, files and all. Photos waiting for approval aren't counted. Photos are shrunk on upload, so they load fast on phones.
