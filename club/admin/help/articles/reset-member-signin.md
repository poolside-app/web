---
title: "How do I help a member who can't sign in?"
category: "Signups & Families"
---

Members don't have passwords. They type their cell number or email on the sign-in page, and Poolside sends them a 6-digit code.

### Check what's on file

1. Go to **Members → Households** and open their family.
2. Compare the phone and email under **Members** with what they're typing.

The most common problem is a different email, like signing up with a Gmail address and typing a work one. Fix it, tap **Save changes**, and have them try again.

### Tell them how to sign in

> "Open the app, or go to the club's page and tap sign in. Type your cell number or email, tap Continue, and enter the 6-digit code we send you. Check your spam folder if an email code doesn't arrive in a minute or two."

### Other people in the family

Each adult with their own phone number or email on the family can sign in on their own. Kids don't need to sign in.

> Tip: A cell number is the most reliable way in. Codes by text arrive in seconds and never go to spam.
