---
title: "How do I open the gate or a door from my phone?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

If your club opens doors from the app, every board member has a button for each door at the top of **Home**.

### Open a door

Tap the door's button. It says when the door has opened, usually within a second or two.

### Who can open what

- Board members can open every door that can be opened remotely, whenever the Board level allows.
- Members only ever open the front gate from their app, during Member hours.
- Doors that are keyfob only, like restrooms, never get a button.

### If it doesn't open

If the gate computer at the pool is offline, the button says so and nothing opens. Try again in a few minutes. If it stays offline, the computer at the pool may need restarting. Let Poolside know through **Still need help?**.

> Tip: Every unlock is logged with who tapped it. Recent ones are on **Settings → Gate & check-in** under **Recent unlocks (last 25)**.
