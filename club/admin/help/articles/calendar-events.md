---
title: "How do I add an event or a closure to the calendar?"
category: "Calendar & Parties"
---

### Add an event

1. Go to **Calendar → Events**.
2. Tap **+ New event**.
3. Fill in the **Title**, pick the **Kind**, and set **Starts** (and **Ends (optional)**). Tick **All-day event** for a whole day.
4. For something that happens every week, set **Repeat**.
5. Tap **Save**.

Members see it on their calendar and under "Coming up".

### Close the pool for a day

Add an event with the kind **🚫 Closure**. On that day, the member app shows "Closed today" instead of the hours.

### Bring in another calendar

If your swim team or another group keeps a Google or SwimTopia calendar, tap **+ Add a calendar** and paste its public calendar link. Its events show on your calendar in their own color.

> Tip: Use **Month** to see the whole month, or **List** to see events in order.
