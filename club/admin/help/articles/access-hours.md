---
title: "How do I change member hours and which doors they open?"
category: "Keyfobs & the Gate"
---

Access levels decide which doors each kind of fob opens, and when. Most clubs have two: Member and Board.

### Change a level

1. Go to **Members → Keyfobs & Access → Access**.
2. On the level you want, tap **Change**, then set:
   - **Doors**: which doors it opens.
   - **Days**: which days of the week.
   - **Hours (leave both empty for any time)**.
   - The two dates under the hours: the first and last day the level works, like opening and closing day.
3. Under **Party hosts**, pick the doors a paid party gets for the day.
4. Tap **Save access**.

### What follows these hours

- The member app's unlock button follows the Member level right away. Outside its hours, members see why the pool is closed and when it opens.
- Keyfobs follow them once the doors are updated. See "What does Sync keyfobs now do?"

> Tip: Set the Member level's last day to your closing day, and member fobs stop on their own once the doors are updated.
