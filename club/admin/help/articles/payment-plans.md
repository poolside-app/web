---
title: "How do payment plans work?"
category: "Money"
---

A family can pay in full, or choose how much to pay today ($0 is fine) and the month to be paid off by. Poolside splits the rest into even monthly payments on their card.

### Card only

Payment plans are always paid by card. The first payment is part of signup, and every later payment is charged to the card they saved. Nobody can mark a plan payment paid by Venmo, check or cash.

### Set it up

Go to **Money → Payments → Money setup → Payment plans**:

- **Enable payment plans** turns the choice on for families.
- **First deadline** and **Paid by then (%)**: for example, half paid by April 10.
- **Paid in full by**: the last day the plan must be finished.
- **Smallest payment ($)** and **Most payments allowed**.
- **Billing day of the month**: the day every plan payment is charged.
- **Gate and key fob start**: when a family on a plan can get in: once half is paid, after the first payment, or as soon as a card is saved.
- **Reactivation fee ($)**: what a family pays to restart a plan that ended.

Tap **Save plan config**.

### Every charge is approved first

No plan payment is charged until someone on the board approves it on **Money → Upcoming**. See "How do I approve this month's plan payments?"

### When a card is declined

Whoever gets payment alerts (set on **Settings → Board**) gets a to-do and a pop-up right away: whose payment, how much and why. Poolside tries the card again 3, 7 and 14 days later and asks the family to update their card; you're told again if the last try fails. A failed card doesn't cancel anything, and the family keeps their keyfob.

### When a deadline is missed

A plan ends only when a family misses a payment deadline: a payment due by your first deadline or your paid-in-full date is still unpaid after it. Then the family's gate access and keyfob stop at once, and they're told how to come back by paying what's overdue plus the reactivation fee. A family who taps **Cancel plan** stops right away too. Under **Families on a payment plan**, **Send reactivation link** sends them that link again. You set the deadlines on **Money → Payments → Money setup**; paid in full by opening day means nobody swims before they've paid.

> Tip: Families see their plan in the app under **My family**: what's paid, what's left and the next charge.
