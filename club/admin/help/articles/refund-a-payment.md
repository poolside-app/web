---
title: "How do I refund a payment?"
category: "Money"
---

### Card payments

Refunds happen in your club's Stripe account, because that's where the money is.

1. Sign in at dashboard.stripe.com.
2. Find the payment by the family's name, amount or date.
3. Choose Refund, pick the full amount or part of it, and confirm.

Poolside hears about the refund from Stripe and gives whoever handles payments a to-do. Nothing changes in Poolside by itself: if the family is leaving, open them on **Members → Households**, untick **Dues paid for current year**, and tap **Save changes**. That also turns off their gate access. For a party or a keyfob, cancel it on its own page. The money goes back to their card in 5 to 10 business days.

A chargeback (the family disputes the charge with their bank) works the same way, with an urgent to-do: Stripe holds the money until you answer it in your Stripe account.

### Venmo, check or cash

Send the money back the same way, then update Poolside: on **Members → Households**, open the family, untick **Dues paid for current year**, and tap **Save changes**.

### Referral rewards

A referral reward goes back to the member's card from **💌 Referral rewards**. See "How does refer-a-friend work?"

> Tip: Stripe keeps its processing fee on a refunded payment. That's Stripe's rule, not Poolside's.
