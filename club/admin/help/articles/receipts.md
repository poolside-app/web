---
title: "Do families get a receipt?"
category: "Money"
---

Every card payment gets a PDF receipt, emailed to the family with the email that payment already sends: the welcome email for a new signup, "Renewal confirmed", each payment-plan payment, "Party booked", and a short "Payment receipt" email for keyfobs, programs and donations. A new family's welcome email has two PDFs: their signed membership form and their receipt.

Each receipt has a number, like 2027-0001, counting up through the season. It shows the club's name and address, the day it was paid, who paid, what it was for, the card fee, the total, the card it was charged to, and for a payment plan what's left to pay.

### The club's address

The receipt shows the address you enter on **Settings → Club info**. Change it there, and new receipts use the new one.

### Venmo payments

There's no receipt for Venmo. Wherever a family pays by Venmo, Poolside tells them the board confirms Venmo payments and their Venmo receipt is their receipt.

### Sending a copy again

When Google Drive is connected, every receipt is saved in the family's own folder: Poolside Archive → your club → Families → their folder, named like "Smith - 123 Oak St", next to their signed membership form. Open it there and email or share the PDF.
