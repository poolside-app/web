---
title: "How do party requests work?"
category: "Calendar & Parties"
---

Members request parties in the app. They pick a date and start time, and the party runs your club's set length.

### Party settings

On **Calendar → Parties**, under **Party settings**, set how long every party runs, the fee, and whether to **Approve open times automatically**. Tap **Save**.

With automatic approval, an open time is the family's on the spot, and paying by card books it right away. Two parties can share a day but not the same hours.

### Reviewing a request

1. Open the request.
2. To change something, fill in **Approve with changes (optional)**: a **New start time**, **New location**, a different **Party fee**, or a **Note to the member (they'll see this)**.
3. Tap **Approve →**, or **Reject**.

### Payment

- By card: the party is booked as soon as they pay.
- By Venmo: check Venmo, then tap **✓ Verify Venmo + book**.
- An unpaid party gives its time back after 2 days.

### When a party is booked

The family gets the party doors (like the snack shack) from opening to closing that day, from their app. Whoever handles parties gets a to-do with the family's contact details.

To cancel, open it and tap **Cancel booking**.
