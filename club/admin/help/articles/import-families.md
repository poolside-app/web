---
title: "How do I import families from a spreadsheet?"
category: "Signups & Families"
---

When your club moves to Poolside, you can bring last season's families in from a spreadsheet instead of typing them.

### Import the file

1. Save your list from Excel or Google Sheets as a CSV file.
2. Go to **Members → Households** and tap **📤 Import CSV**.
3. **Upload your spreadsheet**.
4. **Match your columns to Poolside fields**: tell Poolside which column is the family name, email, phone, address and so on.
5. **Review what we'll import**. Rows that can't be imported, like a family with no email or phone, are listed with the reason. Fix them in your file and upload again, or skip them.
6. Tap **Import valid rows →**.

### Invite them

After the import, tap **Send invites & track sign-ups →**. Each family gets their own link to check their details, sign the policies and pay. The page shows who has finished and who hasn't, and you can send a reminder to the rest.

> Tip: Every family needs an email or a mobile number, or Poolside has no way to send them their link.
