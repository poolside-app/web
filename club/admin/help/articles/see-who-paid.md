---
title: "How do I see who's paid and who hasn't?"
category: "Money"
---

### Families by dues

Go to **Members → Households**. The counts at the top show how many families are paid, on a payment plan, and unpaid. Tap one to see just those families.

### Who owes money right now

1. Go to **Money → Payments**.
2. Open **💰 Waiting for payment**.

It lists everyone with an open balance, oldest first, and anyone who says they paid by Venmo and is waiting to be checked. Narrow it with **Dues**, **Memberships** or **Programs**.

### Payment plans

Families on a plan are listed under **Families on a payment plan**, in **Money setup** on **Money → Payments**. Each one shows what they've paid, what's left, the next charge, and whether they're current or past due.

### A spreadsheet

On **Members → Households**, tap **📥 Export CSV** for a spreadsheet of every family and whether they've paid.

> Tip: The banner on **Home** shows how many families have paid this season, at a glance.
