---
title: "How do late fees work?"
category: "Money"
---

Late fees are off unless you turn them on.

### Set them up

On **Money → Payments → Money setup → Late fees**:

1. Tick **Charge late fees**.
2. Set **Dues are due by**, the **Grace period (days)**, and the **Late fee amount**.
3. Tap **Save late fee settings**.

A family who still hasn't paid after the due date and grace period gets one late fee for the season, never more than one.

### Waiving one

Tap **Waive** on the fee. No questions asked.

> Tip: Poolside keeps 5% of a late fee that actually gets paid. Fees you waive cost nothing.
