---
title: "How do I answer a member's question?"
category: "Signups & Families"
---

Members ask the board questions from the app, under **Ask the board**: a keyfob that won't work, a question about dues, a party. Each question goes to the board member whose position handles that topic.

### Who gets which questions

Go to **Content → Member help** and open **Who handles what**. Each topic goes to whoever holds the position that's told about it (set under **Gets told about** on **Settings → Board**). If nobody holds that position, it goes to the President.

### Answer a question

1. You get a pop-up, and it shows under **Needs you** on **Home**.
2. Open it, or go to **Content → Member help**.
3. Tap **I'm on it** so the rest of the board knows it's handled.
4. Type your answer and tap **Send reply**, or **Send & mark solved** if that settles it.

The member gets your reply as a pop-up in their app, or by email if pop-ups aren't on.

### Photos

Members can attach a photo, like a broken fob or a leak. Tap it to see it full size.

> Tip: Mark questions solved when they're done. Open ones stay on your **Home** until you do.
