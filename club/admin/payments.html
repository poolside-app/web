Card-processing surcharge

When on, members who pay by card see a slightly higher total that covers Stripe's processing fee (~2.9% + 30¢). Venmo / check / cash pricing stays exactly the base price. Disclosure is shown to the member at checkout so the choice is explicit.

On a $600 dues bill, members would see $618.23 for card vs $600.00 for Venmo. The exact pass-through is computed from the rates Stripe charges your club.

🗓️ Payment plans

Families pay in full, or choose how much to pay today ($0 is fine) and the month to be paid off by. Poolside splits the rest evenly by month, only accepts plans that meet your deadlines, and charges the saved card on each date. A card that keeps failing, or a plan not paid in full by your last deadline, cancels the membership until the family reinstates.

How much of the season's dues you need in hand, and by when. Month and day only — these repeat every season, so you set them once. Most clubs want a chunk by the day they open and the rest a couple of months later.

A declined card is tried again 3, 7 and 14 days later. The family gets one email when it first fails; the Treasurer gets one email if it still hasn't gone through.

🧪 Test payments

While on, card checkout opens a fake payment page instead of Stripe, and Venmo applicants get a "Simulate Venmo payment" button. Simulated payments run every automation a real one does — approval, household dues, welcome emails and texts, task closing — but no money moves. Works without Stripe connected. Simulated card payments have a session id starting with sim_.

💌 Referral rewards

A member earns a reward when a family joins with their link and pays. It unlocks 30 days after that payment, then a board member with the payments permission approves it (never for their own family). The member chooses credit toward their next dues or a refund.
