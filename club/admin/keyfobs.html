Keyfobs & Access

Families ask for fobs in the app. A new family's first fob is free; another fob, or a replacement, is paid for in the app before it shows up here. To issue one, type the number printed on it and hand it over; Keyfob sync, below, puts it on the doors. A lost fob comes off the doors at the next sync. Member fobs open the doors only while the season is open and the family has paid; board fobs work all year.

Keyfob sync

Puts Poolside's keyfobs on the doors: new fobs, turned-off and lost fobs, and the hours and dates above. It checks first and shows you what would change; nothing is written until you tap Send. Cards Poolside doesn't manage are never touched, and the doors keep working while it runs.

Settings

Paying by card in the app adds the card fee for the member, so the club keeps the whole fee. Fobs added at signup or renewal are paid with the dues. Families renewing never get a free fob. The most per family counts the free one; lost and turned-off fobs don't count.
