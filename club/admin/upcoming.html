Payment-plan payments are only charged once someone here ticks them. Every plan payment falls on the club's billing day (the 1st, set on Payments setup). Ticking a payment also lets it be tried again 3, 7 and 14 days later if the card is declined. Payments a family makes itself, like the first payment at signup, aren't listed.

🧪 Test payments are on. Plans paid with a test card are never charged; use "Simulate the next payment" on Payments to move them along.
