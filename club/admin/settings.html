▶ 📁 Backups & archive — Google Drive auto-archive of every application

When connected, every new application is automatically archived to your club's Google Drive: a frozen PDF of the signed waiver gets saved into a folder for the current year (e.g. "2026 Sign-ups"), and a new row is added to your master roster spreadsheet. One-way only — deleting something in this app never deletes it from your Drive.
