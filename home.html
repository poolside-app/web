Plain English. No "modules" or "add-ons" you have to upgrade for. Every feature works on every plan — tiers only differ on how many households fit.

Magic-link by email or SMS. Once a member signs in, they stay signed in — sessions roll forward on every visit so no one's locked out mid-summer.

One screen to set up the apply form: heading, tiers, policies, emails, season toggle. New applicants land in a pipeline; Stripe-paid auto-approve.

Track families, not just individuals. Primary contact adds household members from their phone, signs waivers, transfers the role when needed.

Always-visible card on the home page with progress bar + Donate button. Stripe donations auto-bump the bar in real-time. Recent supporters list under it.

Sponsor program

Sell sponsor slots — logos rotate in a strip on the home page, and an optional pop-up cycles through them on app open.

💬

Anonymous feedback

One-tap card on every home page. Members report a broken tile, a dirty bathroom, a complaint — with a photo, no name attached.

For clubs with a compatible keyfob panel. Members unlock from their phone with a full audit log on your end. MENGQI HXC-7000 verified; others case-by-case.

Members upload pool photos, admin approves before they appear. Auto-rotating home-page carousel with tap-to-enlarge lightbox + swipe.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Can we just try it before signing up?

Yes — open bishopestates.poolsideapp.com on your phone. It's a real working club running on Poolside. Tap around the public landing, the apply form, the photo gallery, the donation flow — everything you'd see if you were a member at your own club.

Do members need to download anything from an app store?

No. Poolside is a progressive web app — they open the link on their phone and tap "Add to Home Screen" once. After that it looks and feels like a native app, with your custom club icon, no browser bars, and no App Store gatekeeping.

How does the donation thermometer work?

Members donate by card (Stripe) or Venmo. Card donations auto-bump the thermometer the moment the payment clears. Venmo donations come in via the admin's "Record manual donation" button, which also bumps the bar. Donor names + messages render under the bar in a GoFundMe-style "Recent supporters" list (when donors opt in).

What about the gate? How does that work?

It's a paid add-on for clubs with a compatible keyfob panel. We've certified the MENGQI-CONTROL HXC-7000 end-to-end. For other panels, you submit your model and we evaluate one by one — on-site testing required for new vendors. Verified panels run $250 setup + $25/month. We never promise a panel we haven't tested.

Where does our data live? Can we get it out?

Your data lives in our Supabase Postgres database, mirrored to your Google Drive folder as PDFs (signed waivers) and a Sheet (master roster). You own the Drive folder. If you ever cancel, you keep all of it. The mirror is one-way — deleting in Poolside never deletes from your Drive.

What if our board volunteers aren't tech-y?

That's exactly who this is for. The admin pages avoid jargon ("slug," "endpoint," "token") wherever a board member would see them. Help articles substitute your real club name and URL. Setup is a wizard, not a manual.

What happens when the treasurer rotates?

The new treasurer creates an admin account, the old one gets deactivated, every signed waiver / payment record / household roster is exactly where the old one left it. No "where did Karen save the spreadsheet" archaeology.

Money-back guarantee?

If you're not happy in the first 30 days of a paid plan, email doug.frevele@gmail.com and we'll refund the subscription. No "exit interview." We'd rather you find the right tool than feel stuck.
