Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Do members need to download an app?

No. Poolside is a progressive web app — members open the link on their phone and tap "Add to Home Screen" once. After that it looks and feels like a native app, with your custom club icon, no browser bars, and no App Store / Play Store gatekeeping.

How does the gate unlock work?

It's a paid add-on — not bundled with the base subscription, because keyfob panels vary wildly between vendors. We've certified the MENGQI-CONTROL HXC-7000 end-to-end. For other panels, you submit your model and we evaluate one-by-one — on-site testing is required for new ones. Verified panels: $250 setup + $25/month. Custom integrations are quoted individually before any work starts.

How does the donation thermometer work?

Set a goal in Settings → Fundraiser. When members donate by card via Stripe, the webhook auto-inserts a verified donation row and the thermometer recomputes. For Venmo / cash / check, your admin records the donation in Members → Donations and the bar moves the moment they save. Donor names and messages render under the bar (GoFundMe-style) when they opt in.

How do members pay dues?

However your club already does it. Stripe (auto-charge a saved card, instant approval), Venmo (admin verifies the payment), PayPal (link-based), check (manual). All four buttons appear automatically based on what you have configured. Stripe's the only one that supports auto-renewal; the rest you mark paid by hand.

Where does our data live? Can we get it out?

Your data lives in our Supabase Postgres database, mirrored to your Google Drive folder as PDFs (signed waivers) and a Sheet (master roster). You own the Drive folder. If you ever cancel, you keep all of that. The mirror is one-way, append-only — deleting something in Poolside never deletes from your Drive.

Is there a free trial?

The Free Forever tier IS the free trial — no time limit, no card, every feature included, capped at 20 households. Outgrow that and the paid tiers offer 14 days no-card before billing.

Can we use our own domain?

Yes — included in the Enterprise tier. Point yourclub.com at us, we handle the SSL. You'll still get an admin login at the standard yourclub.poolsideapp.com in case DNS ever flakes.

What if our board volunteers aren't tech-y?

That's exactly who we built it for. The admin pages avoid every piece of jargon we could find — no "slugs," "endpoints," "tokens" anywhere a board member would see. Help articles substitute your actual club name and URL so nothing reads like fill-in-the-blanks. Setup is a wizard, not a manual.

What happens when the treasurer rotates?

The new treasurer creates an admin account in 30 seconds, the old one gets deactivated, and every signed waiver / payment record / household roster is exactly where the old one left it. No "where did Karen save the spreadsheet" archaeology.
