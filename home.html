What's actually inside.

Plain English. No "modules" or "add-ons" you have to upgrade for. Every feature works on every plan — tiers only differ on how many households fit.

💸

Recurring auto-pay

The big one. Card on file, charged each year automatically. ~85% of members set this up and you never chase them again. Stripe deposits to your bank — we never touch the money.

📅

Payment plans → more members

Families who can't drop $500 in March can split into 4 monthly chunks. Each installment auto-charges. You'll add members who'd otherwise say no.

📨

Auto reminders, auto close

When someone's late, the app texts + emails them on a schedule — 3 days, 7 days, 14 days. After a final warning, their gate code stops working and their account goes inactive. You only chase the 2–3 people who actually want to talk.

🎉

Party booking in 3 taps

Member requests Saturday → you tap Approve → they pay → the day locks on the club calendar. Members can't double-book a date that's already taken. Auto party-rules acceptance built in (you write them once).

📱

Text-message sign-in

No passwords for Grandma to forget. Member types their phone → gets a 6-digit code → they're in. iOS and Android even auto-fill the code from the text.

🔔

Push to the right board member

Install Poolside on your phone (like a real app). Treasurer gets payment alerts. Party chair gets party requests. Gate manager gets gate alerts. Nobody gets buzzed about things that aren't theirs.

🌐

Your own URL

Members visit yourclub.poolsideapp.com — live the second you sign up. Put it in your email blast, your Facebook, on a flyer with a QR code.

👨‍👩‍👧

Members manage themselves

They sign their own waivers. They add their kids. They update their phone number when it changes. They submit photos. They pay their dues. You stop being IT support.

Members tap their phone to unlock the gate. Full audit log. $250 setup + $25/mo. Works with the MENGQI-CONTROL panel; others case-by-case. (Skip this if you have a key-from-the-board manual gate.)

Stripe's optional. Stick with Venmo and the app still tracks who paid, sends reminders, and runs the rest of the club — just with a manual "yes I got the Venmo" tap from the treasurer. No lock-in.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked.

Is it actually free up to 20 households?

Yes. No credit card needed to sign up, no time limit, no feature paywalls. If your club stays at 20 or fewer households forever, you stay free forever. We'll cover the ~$5/yr it costs us to keep your club running because (a) tiny clubs become loyal advocates and (b) when you grow past 20, we'd like to be the ones you upgrade with.

What's a "payment plan" — why does it matter?

Annual dues are a chunk. A family looking at "$500 in March" might say no when "$125 per month, March–June" would be fine. Payment plans let you offer both. Each installment auto-charges the card on file. Most clubs see membership grow 10–20% the year they turn this on.

What happens when someone doesn't pay?

The app texts AND emails them on a schedule — 3 days late, 7 days, 14 days. After your final warning (configurable), their gate code stops working and their member home shows "renew to come back." You don't lift a finger except for the 2–3 people who actually want to talk it through.

Can we keep using Venmo?

Yep. Stripe is optional. With Venmo: members pay your @clubname handle, click "I paid" in the app, your treasurer confirms with one tap. The app still tracks who paid, sends reminders, and runs the rest of the club. Just no auto-recurring (Venmo doesn't support it). Most clubs offer both and let members pick.

Can Grandma Suzy the treasurer really run this?

That's literally the design brief. Admin pages avoid jargon ("slug," "endpoint," "JWT" — banned). Setup is a checklist on the dashboard, not a manual. Help articles substitute your real club name + URL. When something breaks, plain-English error messages tell you what to do next, not stack traces.

Do members install an app from the App Store?

No app store. Members open yourclub.poolsideapp.com on their phone, tap "Add to Home Screen" once, and it looks and feels like a real app with your club's icon. No App Store delays, no Apple/Google fees, no install friction. Same for board members on the admin side.

How do board members get notified — and not spammed?

Install Poolside on your phone, turn on push. Each board member has a role (treasurer, party chair, gate manager, etc.) and only gets buzzed about things matching their role. The party chair sees party requests; the treasurer sees payment alerts. The president can opt into everything or nothing.

What about the gate?

Paid add-on. Members tap their phone at the gate, the app pings a compatible keyfob panel, gate opens. $250 setup + $25/mo. We've certified MENGQI-CONTROL panels; others case-by-case. Most clubs skip this for year 1 — manual keys still work fine, and the rest of Poolside is plenty without it.

Where does our data live? Can we get it out?

Your data lives in our Supabase Postgres database AND is mirrored to your Google Drive folder as PDFs (signed waivers) + a Sheet (roster). You own the Drive folder. If you cancel, you keep all of it. The mirror is one-way — deleting in Poolside never deletes from your Drive.

What happens when the treasurer rotates?

New treasurer signs up, old one gets deactivated, every record is exactly where the old treasurer left it. No "where did Karen save the spreadsheet" archaeology. No fighting over Venmo passwords.
